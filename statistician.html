About

Developer who thinks in numbers.

I'm a full-stack developer with a background in applied statistics. That combination means I don't just build features — I build the measurement into them from day one.

Most of my work lives in PHP and MySQL: custom web apps, dashboards, booking systems, and data pipelines. When a project needs real analysis — A/B tests, forecasting, cohort modeling — I do that too, and hand back results people can actually use.

I'm conversant with a wide range of statistical packages — R, Python, Stata, SPSS, SAS, Minitab, Jamovi, Statistica, Orange, Excel — and pick the right tool for the job rather than forcing one stack onto every problem.

I care about clean code, accessible interfaces, and shipping things that work. If you need someone who can write the query, build the page, and tell you whether the numbers mean anything — that's me.
